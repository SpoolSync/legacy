# Legacy

Part of Pixora. One place for what the people you trust would need: where your will and papers are, who your executor and powers of attorney are, insurance and who it pays, accounts and beneficiaries (last 4 digits only), bills to settle, digital accounts, wishes and letters.

- A trusted person can ask to open it. You're told in every Pixora app and on Pixora Today, and you have a waiting period (1–90 days) to deny. You can also let them in right away, or take it back.
- Their copy is encrypted on your phone with a key sealed to their phone's key. The server only enforces *when* they can fetch it; it can't read it, and neither can Pixora or any AI.
- Warns if something looks like a full account, card or SIN number, or a password.
- Not legal advice, and it doesn't replace a signed will.

Live: https://spoolsync.github.io/legacy/
